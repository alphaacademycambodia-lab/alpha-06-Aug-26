Flexible learning options

Four ways we teach

In-home tutoring, office-based sessions, online classes and small groups — chosen to fit different preferences, distances and timetables.

At your home

The tutor travels to you. Familiar surroundings, no commute, and parents can see the progress first-hand.

Live online

Video lessons with a shared whiteboard and screen sharing. Ideal for provinces, expats and busy schedules.

At our office

A quiet, structured study space with materials on hand — good for focused exam preparation.

Small groups

Two to four students at a similar level. More affordable, and some students learn better alongside peers.

Service 01

Home Tutoring

A vetted tutor comes to your home for focused one-to-one lessons — the most direct way to lift a struggling subject.

  • Undivided attention. No classroom of forty — every question gets answered on the spot.
  • Your schedule. After school, evenings, weekends or holiday intensives.
  • Homework support. Lessons align with what the school is actually teaching that week.
  • Parent visibility. Regular feedback so you know what improved and what still needs work.

Questions

Frequently asked

How much do lessons cost?

Rates depend on the subject, the level, the format (home, office or online) and how many sessions per week. Because every plan is built around the individual student, we quote after the free consultation — so you only pay for what your child actually needs. Call +855 93 792 767 for current rates.

Which areas do you cover for home tutoring?

We cover Phnom Penh and surrounding areas for in-home sessions. If you're outside our travel radius, online classes give you exactly the same tutor and curriculum — just delivered over video.

How quickly can lessons start?

Most families are matched with a tutor and have their first session within a week of the consultation. Urgent exam preparation can usually be arranged faster.

Can lessons be taught in Khmer, English or Chinese?

Yes. Our tutors are proficient across Khmer, English and Chinese, and will teach in whichever language the student learns fastest in — including switching between them within a lesson.

What do I need for an online class?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a camera, a stable internet connection, and somewhere quiet to sit. We'll send the link and a short setup guide before the first session.

Do you teach adults, not just school students?

Yes. A good share of our students are working adults studying English or Mandarin for their careers, or completing academic catch-up. Sessions are scheduled around work hours.

What if my child doesn't connect with the tutor?

Tell us. The match matters as much as the qualifications, and we'll reassign a different tutor without fuss.

Not sure which option fits?

That's exactly what the free consultation is for. One short conversation and we'll recommend the format, subject plan and schedule.
