About Alpha Academy Cambodia

Who we are

Personal teaching, in a comfortable environment

Alpha Academy Cambodia is a dedicated home and online tutoring service committed to enhancing students' academic performance across various subjects and languages.

We offer personalised tutoring sessions tailored to meet the unique needs of each student, ensuring effective learning in a comfortable environment. That last part matters: a student who is anxious about being wrong in front of forty classmates will ask a question in their own living room — and that question is usually where the progress starts.

Our tutors work with the school's actual curriculum rather than a parallel syllabus, so lessons reinforce what's being taught this week while quietly repairing the foundations that were missed last year.

What drives us

Mission, vision and values

Our mission

To raise the academic performance of every student we teach through personalised, patient instruction — regardless of where they start.

Our vision

A Cambodia where quality one-to-one teaching is a normal option for every family — not a privilege reserved for a few.

Our values

Patience over pressure. Honesty about progress. Respect for the student's pace, language and confidence.

Why choose us

Four reasons families stay with Alpha

These aren't slogans — they're the four things parents tell us made the difference.

Experienced educators

Our team comprises qualified tutors with years of teaching experience, ensuring quality instruction. Every tutor is interviewed, subject-tested and observed before being placed with a family.

Customised learning plans

We assess each student's strengths and areas for improvement to create personalised learning strategies — then revise the plan as the student moves, rather than following it blindly to the end of term.

Convenient scheduling

We offer flexible scheduling to fit into your busy lifestyle, making learning accessible and stress-free. Evenings, weekends and school-holiday intensives are all normal for us.

Proven track record

Many of our students have shown significant improvement in their academic performance and exam results — often within a single term of consistent weekly sessions.

Our approach

How a student's term actually runs

Structure is what turns tutoring into results. Here's the cycle every student goes through.

Diagnose

A short assessment finds the real gap — which is often two grades below the topic the student is currently failing.

Plan

We set a term goal, break it into weekly targets and pick the language of instruction that suits the student best.

Teach & practise

Weekly sessions of explanation, guided practice and independent work, with homework matched to the week's target.

Review & report

Regular checkpoints against the plan, plus honest feedback to parents on what improved and what hasn't yet.

Our tutors

Who will be teaching your child

Every Alpha tutor clears the same four checks before they meet a student.

  • Qualified in their subject. Relevant degree or teaching background — verified, not assumed.
  • Proven classroom experience. Years of teaching, not a first-time tutor practising on your child.
  • Language proficiency. Able to teach in Khmer, English or Chinese as the student needs.
  • The right temperament. Patient with slow progress, and honest with parents about it.
